Ask us on WhatsApp ↗
How does WEDDINGS work?

Tell us about your wedding, choose a package and style, then work with us to personalize and approve your invitation before sharing it.

Can I customize my invitation?

Yes. Customization options depend on your selected package. Share the look and details you have in mind when you enquire.

Can I use my own photos?

Yes, your own photos can be considered as part of the design, subject to your package and image quality.

Can guests RSVP online?

RSVP functionality is part of the planned digital experience. We’ll confirm availability and setup for your package before booking.

Can I add Google Maps?

Venue directions or a Google Maps link can be included when you provide the correct venue location.

Can I include a Wedding Talk?

Yes. Tell us what ceremony information you’d like guests to see, and we’ll discuss the best way to present it.

Can I include a reception?

Yes. You can share reception timing, venue details and other helpful information.

Can I change information after publishing?

Ask us about update options for your package before booking. Some changes may require additional work.

Can I share the invitation through WhatsApp?

Yes. Once your invitation is published, you can share its link through WhatsApp.

How long does it take to create my invitation?

Timing depends on the package, your content and the number of revisions. We’ll agree on a timeline when we discuss your wedding.
